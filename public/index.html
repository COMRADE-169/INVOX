Powerful Capabilities

Everything You Need for Effortless Billing

Engineered from the ground up to eliminate tedious invoicing headaches for freelancers, agencies, and vendors.

Live A4 Invoice Preview

Real-time two-way synchronization. As you type, watch your branded A4 invoice update instantly with 5 designer color themes, logos, amount in words, and bank credentials.

Full Purchase Orders (PO)

End-to-end procurement module. Track vendor orders with status workflows (Draft, Sent, Approved, Received, Closed) and convert approved POs to invoices with a single click.

Embedded UPI QR Code

Zero payment friction in India. Invoices automatically generate dynamic UPI QR codes populated with the exact grand total and invoice reference for instant mobile payment via GPay or PhonePe.

GST & Global Currencies

Automated Indian GST split (CGST + SGST 50/50 intra-state or IGST inter-state) and international currency support (INR ₹, USD $, EUR €, GBP £, AED, CAD, AUD, SGD, JPY).

High-Res PDF & Clean Print

Client-side rendering via html2pdf generates crystal-clear A4 PDFs with zero watermarks. Dedicated @media print styles ensure border-to-border printing straight from your browser.
